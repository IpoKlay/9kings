StarNinjas — 20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)
https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
Original sword_clash.1.ogg, .2.ogg, .3.ogg. Cut, filtered, lowered slightly, layered with impacts.

Kenney — Impact Sounds
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Selected metal, wood, punch, plate, soft, grass-footstep recordings.
Cut, equalized, layered and normalized; tactile UI sounds derived from the same recordings.

artisticdude — Battle Sound Effects (submitted by Ogrebane)
https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
Bow.wav, swish_2.wav, swish_3.wav, swish_4.wav. CC0 license option selected.
Cut, equalized and normalized.

HaelDB — Male Grunt/Yelling sounds
https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds
3grunt1.wav, 3grunt2.wav, 3grunt3.wav. CC0 license option selected.
Brief vocal efforts, filtered and layered at different timings/pitches.

StarNinjas — Crowd Shouting/Speaking Ambience
https://opengameart.org/content/crowd-shoutingspeaking-ambience
Short excerpt mixed quietly under the rally vocals.

No machine-generated voices. No long reverb tails. Original downloads are not required at runtime.
audio-bank.js contains 24 kHz mono PCM16 versions for offline/browser compatibility.
The audio/ folder contains the processed WAV files and a validation manifest.

v0.5 UI cues: original synthesized triangle/sine note sequences layered with the
credited CC0 wood ticks. These are original cues; no Vampire Survivors audio is
included. The combat recording edits are unchanged from v0.4.
