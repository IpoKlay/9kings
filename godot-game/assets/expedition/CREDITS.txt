Long itinerary expansion (2026-10-23):
Place names and coordinates continue to use the same Pleiades export (CC BY 3.0).
Per-place URLs, source titles and Barrington Atlas references are retained in
data/expedition_sources.json; contributor attribution is available at those URLs.
The original route display was schematic. The 2026-10-23 geographic atlas uses
the existing place coordinates, with marker offsets and site leaders for clarity.
Three theaters of fifteen
stops are a gameplay structure, not an attested 30 BCE campaign. Oea hinterland
is an abstract encounter anchored to Oea, not a separate ancient settlement.

Geographic atlas: atlas.png, 2880x1260 pixel texture generated offline from the
same Natural Earth geometry and palette. All 55 anchors are listed in
data/expedition_atlas_positions.json; routes are unchanged fictional itineraries.
