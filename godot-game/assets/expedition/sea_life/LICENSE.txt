BONUS
RenderMapToPNG.cs is the script I use to export the final maps from Godot without having to screenshot. It's pretty clunky, so I'm only including it as a bonus and I don't provide support for this script.
I only know C# and not GDScript so it's a C# script needing the Godot Mono version, sorry for this. You do not need Godot Mono to use the rest of the pack, it is only required for this specific script.
The variables FrameCount and FrameDelayMs are used to export multiples frames with a delay between them. 
The animations are animated with 200ms per frame. The waves animation consists of 10 frames of 200ms (waves) and 1 frame of 800 ms (or 4 x 200ms) for the cooldown time with no waves.
I usually export with FrameCount higher than 14 and FrameDelayMs lower than 200 because it's not very precise on the delays.
