Latin, numerals and supported symbols:
m5x7 by Daniel Linssen (managore), CC0.
User-supplied source: /Users/ip/Desktop/9king/字体/m5x7.ttf
Official source: https://managore.itch.io/m5x7
Credits and license: M5X7-CREDITS.txt
m5x7.ttf is an unmodified source copy.
m5x7-eagle.ttf is a CC0 derivative named m5x7 Eagle.
Only em size (1024 -> 640) and family
names were adjusted to align its rendered size with the Chinese companion;
glyph shapes, advances and vertical metrics remain in their source units.
Reproducible build: tools/prepare_pixel_font.py --m5x7 (offline, requires fontTools).
The previous Monogram italic font remains available but is not selected.

Additional symbol fallback:
Monogram Extended by Vinícius Menézio (@vmenezio), CC0.
Official source: https://datagoblin.itch.io/monogram
Attribution: MONOGRAM-CREDITS.txt
monogram-eagle.ttf supplies characters absent from both m5x7 and Fusion Pixel,
including the minus sign and check mark. Its glyphs are unchanged, with em
1024 -> 640 and family names adjusted; rebuild with tools/prepare_pixel_font.py.

Runtime:
game_pixel_font.tres combines the fonts into a single shared Godot font.
game_pixel_theme.tres supplies menus, cards, maps, dialogs and tooltips.
Battlefield draw calls use the same font resource. Pixel font imports disable
antialiasing, hinting and system fallback; rendering has no external font or
network dependency. The old Noto Serif resource is retained but not used.
